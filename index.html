Vacuum Burst Lab

Puffs food with a sudden pressure drop instead of oil, using the same physics as 爆米香 but pulling a vacuum rather than building high pressure. Warm food is exposed all at once to a pre-evacuated tank. The water inside boils instantly and puffs the structure. Use this to size the chamber, tank, valve and pump before buying hardware.

    System layout

    Control sequence

    1. EVACUATE Pump the tank down to the target pressure while the chamber lid is open.
    2. LOAD Close the lid; the interlock confirms it is sealed.
    3. HEAT Bring the food core to target temperature at atmospheric pressure (probe + PID).
    4. BURST Open the fast valve; log pressure at 1 kHz for the first second.
    5. DRY Hold the vacuum with gentle heat until the load cell reaches the target moisture, which sets the crunch.
    6. VENT Bleed air back in slowly so the structure doesn't collapse, then unlock the lid.

    Safety interlocks: the lid can't open under vacuum, the heater cuts out above its limit or on a probe fault, there's a pressure relief path, and an emergency stop kills the heater and pump.

    Model assumptions

    Air flows through the valve as an isothermal orifice (choked or subsonic, discharge coefficient 0.7). The water that flashes is the sensible heat above the boiling point at the final pressure, cₚ(T − Tsat)/h_fg, capped by the food's water content. Food cₚ is 3.5 kJ/kg·K and food density 0.8 g/mL. The steam the burst makes is added to the final pressure, so this is a worst case: walls and the pump remove steam in reality. Boiling points come from the Antoine equation. The puff index combines superheat with the ratio of drop time to a 0.15 s vapour-escape time. It is a starting point: fit it to measured expansion ratios from Prototype 1.

    Device protocol

    The ESP32 sends one line per sample over USB serial. Lines starting with D, are data; any other line shows up in the device log above. Send at 1 kHz during the burst and 20–100 Hz otherwise.

    D,<millis>,<chamber kPa>,<tank kPa>,<food °C>,<mass g>,<STATE>
D,152340,101.28,2.95,84.6,150.2,HEAT

// ESP32 (Arduino)
Serial.printf("D,%lu,%.2f,%.2f,%.1f,%.1f,%s\n", millis(), pc, pt, tf, mass, state);

    States: EVACUATE, LOAD, HEAT, BURST, DRY, VENT, IDLE.
